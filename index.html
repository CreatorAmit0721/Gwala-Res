Our Story

A simple idea, done well

Good food, comfortable seating, and enough time to enjoy both. Tucked near the Stadium in Ganga Vihar, Gwala has become the spot where Sahibganj sits under the string lights with family and friends — a plate of momos, a kulhad of chai, no hurry to leave.

The menu stays approachable: snacks, tandoor breads, momos, pasta and litti chokha — all pure veg, all made fresh through the day, whether you're dining in or ordering home.
